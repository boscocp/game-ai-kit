# T52.1 — What a guard perceives, how aware it is, and what it knows

Size: L · Prereqs: T14, T49, T67 · Files: `godot/addons/ai_perception/`, `godot/addons/ai_fuzzy/`, `python/gameaikit/perception/`, `spec/vectors/perception/`, `godot/test/ai_perception/`, `modules/ai_perception.json`, `Makefile`, `docs/concepts/stealth-ai.md`

## Goal

The first half of the owner's T52 (2026-10-21): bots against the player
with an optional stealth, "inspirado no assassins creed, splinter cell e
metal gear", applying what T67 learns. The owner split it in two on the
same day. This half is the techniques, with no level and no demo: what a
guard perceives, how aware that makes it, and what it then knows. T52.2
builds the level, the cooperation, the player and the overlay on them.

The owner's standing rule holds here first, because this is where it is
cheapest to keep: "aqui devemos sempre trabalhar a autonomia e fazer o
mínimo de if else fixo na demo, pra ser uma IA genérica", and the agent
"em outro mapa, outro contexto com outros modelos 3d ... deve saber se
virar". So nothing in this task knows a level: every function takes a walk
graph (`ai_core`'s search port), a light per cell and a percept, and every
number is a data row per guard type.

## Decided (2026-10-21)

- **The research is [`docs/concepts/stealth-ai.md`](../docs/concepts/stealth-ai.md)**
  (wave 74), 20 sources each opened. Perceiving and being aware are two
  things [Walsh 2015]: a vision shape that widens then narrows, read as a
  certainty per zone [McIntosh 2015; Rabin 2015], light at the player's
  feet [Leonard 2003], sound along the level, not through walls [Walsh
  2015; Miles 2013]. Awareness accumulates and decays through every
  intermediate value [McIntosh 2015; Leonard 2003]; the phases are
  thresholds on it, in data [Welsh 2013], with a caution that lasts after
  an alert [Ubisoft 2024]. A guard knows only what it perceived or heard
  [McIntosh 2015]; a shared belief map spreads while nobody looks and is
  wiped where any guard sees [Isla 2013].
- **The awareness rate is an `ai_fuzzy` controller** -- the owner's choice
  of the two offered. It keeps T14's lesson: the 2D suspicion grown from a
  grid to rays at a body.
- **Metal Gear is taken as design only** -- its phases as pacing, the radio,
  the radar, the hiding places -- since no developer source on its AI
  exists. Nothing in this task claims to work as Metal Gear's guards do.
- **No cheat in what a guard knows.** Welsh's 2-3 s of "knowing" and his
  search weight towards the player's real position are left out [Welsh
  2015]; the belief does their job.
- **Off-screen hearing halved is on by default** (the owner asked for the
  sources' recommendation; it is Walsh's): a guard off screen and far
  enough away hears minor events at half the radius, because being heard
  by a guard the player cannot see feels unfair whatever the simulation
  says [Walsh 2015], and the Mirage team aimed at detection players trust
  [Reparaz 2024]. Here it is only a factor the hearing accepts; T52.2 ties
  it to the camera and shows it.

## Steps

1. **Read T67's result.** Its percept record and the parts of
   `ai_perception` it uses are the base; extend them, do not fork them.
2. **The vision shape.** Zones that widen then narrow with distance, each
   with a certainty from 0 to 1; movement adds, poor light and hiding spots
   take away [Rabin 2015]. Rays to a few points on the body; how many must
   be clear is a stance rule. Returns a percept: certainty, which points
   were seen, and why the rest were not -- the overlay's material.
3. **Light** as a value per cell, sampled at the target's feet.
4. **Hearing along the walk graph**: a flood from the source over the
   search port, loudness falling with path length, zero where no path
   reaches. `Hearing`'s straight line stays for the 2D stealth demo; its
   header and this one say which is which. A listener factor of at most 1
   (the off-screen halving) is an argument, never a gain.
5. **Typed events** as data rows: sense, priority, radius, lifetime
   [Miles 2013] -- footstep, shot, thrown object, body, open door, a guard's
   call. A guard's own call is an event like the others.
6. **Awareness.** One number per guard and stimulus, rising at the rate an
   `ai_fuzzy` rule base gives from certainty, light, motion and distance,
   falling at a rate of its own and through every intermediate value. Phase
   thresholds as fractions of the peak; a caution floor and its duration.
   All of it per guard type in a JSON file, as Crysis does [Welsh 2013].
   The phase is a pure function of the number and the row -- there is no
   transition code.
7. **Memory and belief.** Each guard's memory: where, when and from what it
   last perceived the target. A belief map over the walk graph: seeded at
   a perceived position, diffusing along edges each step, zeroed in every
   cell any guard's percept covers, normalised. It is written only from a
   percept or a heard report.
8. **Python twins and vectors** for the shape, the hearing flood, the
   awareness (fuzzy rate included), the phase reading and the belief.
   `make test-perception-vectors`, as `test-teamwork-vectors` is.
9. **Properties**, `godot/test/ai_perception/awareness_properties.gd`, over
   seeded generated graphs, below.

`awareness_properties.gd` holds, over seeded generated graphs and each
with a planted case it must reject (invariant 4):

- **Nothing known that was not perceived.** Awareness does not rise on a
  step with no percept; memory and belief change only from a percept or a
  heard report. Planted: an update that reads the target's true position.
- **Awareness follows exposure.** With all else fixed, the awareness
  reached after a stated time does not fall as the target comes nearer,
  into light, or moves; outside every zone, or with no clear body point,
  it does not rise. Planted: the light input inverted.
- **The phases follow the thresholds.** The phase is the one the number
  and the row give; falling, it passes through every phase in between;
  after an alert it stays at or above the caution floor for the stated
  time. Planted: a threshold read from the wrong row.
- **Sound goes round, not through.** A listener with no path to the source
  hears nothing at any straight-line distance; loudness never rises with
  path length. Planted: Euclidean distance.
- **Off-screen halving only lowers.** With the factor on, no listener
  hears any event louder than with it off. Planted: a factor above 1.
- **The belief is honest.** It is zero in every cell a percept covers,
  sums to one, and moves only along graph edges. Planted: diffusion
  through a wall.
- **Portable.** Every claim above holds on graphs the test generates; no
  script in the addon names a level.
